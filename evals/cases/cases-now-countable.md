# Eval case — JUDGE-CASES-NOW-COUNTABLE

**Feature:** 023-case-resolution · **Scored by:** an independent judge · **Not before:** a judge
exists for `B2`.

## Question

`B2` says the eval cases have never been scored, deferred until an independent judge exists. 023
makes the set resolvable and countable. **Was knowing the count ever the obstacle?**

## What counts as evidence

**Confirmed** — the judge who eventually scores `B2` reports that they could not have started
without a resolvable set: cases that turned out to be missing, or rows binding to the wrong file,
would have been discovered mid-pass and invalidated part of the work.

**Refuted** — the judge says the obstacle was always their own availability, and that three broken
rows out of fourteen would have been obvious on first contact. Then 023 was tidying that named
itself an unblocker, and the honest framing of its value is the *gate* — that the fourth broken row
cannot appear silently — not the cleanup.

## Why it cannot be scored now

`B2` is blocked on a judge. This case is blocked on the same judge, which is uncomfortable and
correct: the claim *"this unblocks B2"* can only be settled by the thing B2 is waiting for.

## Trap

Do not score this on the count being wrong. `B14` claimed 32 rows and 21 unresolvable; the true
figures are 14 and 1. That the backlog was wrong proves nobody had measured — it does not prove
measuring was the blocker. Those are different claims and 023's value depends on the second.
